Government Support

Indian Government Incentives for Solar

  • Accelerated Depreciation: Claim up to 40% depreciation in the first year — massive tax savings
  • Net Metering: Export surplus power to the grid and get credited at retail tariff rates
  • MNRE Subsidies: Up to 40% subsidy for residential installations under PM Surya Ghar
  • GST Benefits: Solar equipment attracts only 5% GST — one of the lowest rates
  • Green Energy Open Access: Reduced cross-subsidy surcharge for solar consumers
  • Priority Lending: Solar projects classified as priority sector by RBI — easier financing

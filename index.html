What We Do

Four areas of renewable focus

Krita works exclusively with industrial facilities, large commercial establishments, and institutional clients. We do not serve the residential segment.

ACTIVE

Solar EPC

End-to-end engineering, procurement, and construction for rooftop and ground-mounted solar projects. We manage the complete lifecycle from site assessment through commissioning.

IN DEVELOPMENT

Battery Management

Battery storage solutions serving wind, solar, green hydrogen, and EV segments. Joint ventures planned for manufacturing near Chennai for industrial-scale deployments.

IN DEVELOPMENT

Wind Turbine Generators

Development of highly efficient, cost-effective WTGs — initially 1 MW and 1.86 MW units for repowering, scaling to 6 MW turbines for IPP deployment.

IN DEVELOPMENT

Solar & Wind IPP Ownership

Development and ownership of independent power producer projects through structured SPVs. Direct green energy sales targeting 700 MW ownership by year five.

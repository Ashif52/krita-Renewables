Who We Are

200+ years of collective expertise

KRITA Renewables (formerly Vistrak Power Private Limited) is led by a senior leadership team with over 200 years of combined experience in concept-to-commissioning and O&M across solar, wind, biomass, and small hydro.

Headquartered in Chennai, India, our team brings a proven track record in product and technology development, project execution, fund raising, and power trading for multi-MW renewable energy projects across India.

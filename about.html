Our Story

A focused mandate for industrial growth

Krita Renewables Private Limited was established with a singular, focused mandate: delivering dependable, high-quality, and scalable clean energy infrastructure strictly for industrial and large commercial clients. We do not serve the residential segment.

Promoted and managed by a senior leadership team with decades of hands-on experience across solar, wind, biomass, and small hydro — including product development, project management, manufacturing, and supply chain — Krita brings institutional-grade capability to every single engagement.

Services & Solutions

Integrated solutions

multi‑asset, single partner

We design and deliver multi‑asset energy systems that work together: solar PV, wind, biomass, and IPP solutions including battery energy storage and grid‑synchronization. Each service is delivered as part of a cohesive energy ecosystem.

Vertical 01

Ground‑Mount / Rooftop Solar

Turnkey ground‑mount systems for industrial parks and large campuses — optimized layout, civil design, and high‑yield module selection. We provide complete solar EPC from site integration to regulatory approvals.

Business Outcome

Maximize yield per acre; shorten payback through optimized BOS & O&M.

Vertical 02

Wind Energy

Site assessment, turbine selection, grid interconnection and long‑term operations for hybrid or standalone wind projects. Repowering solutions for legacy wind installations to maximize capacity factors.

Business Outcome

Diversify generation mix and improve capacity factor across seasons.

Vertical 03

Biomass Energy

Design and build of biomass CHP and steam systems for industrial heat and power needs, with fuel‑supply integration. Our team holds extensive experience established over 35 MW of built and operated biomass plants.

Business Outcome

Convert waste streams into reliable baseload power and reduce fossil fuel spend.

Vertical 04

IPP & Multi‑Asset Ownership Models

Build‑own‑operate (BOO), EaaS, and hybrid financing to match balance‑sheet and tax objectives. Krita owns, operates, and maintains assets to deliver contracted clean energy services over a 25-year cycle.

Business Outcome

Access modern energy without upfront capital or with optimized balance‑sheet treatment.

Vertical 05

Battery Energy Storage & Grid Integration

BESS sizing, control logic, and DG/solar synchronization for demand charge management, black‑start capability, and grid services. Flawless control algorithms that protect industrial operational continuity.

Business Outcome

Reduce peak demand charges, enable time‑shifted energy use, and improve power quality.

Vertical 06

Operations & Performance Services

24/7 remote monitoring, predictive maintenance, performance guarantees, and lifecycle spare‑parts management. Our remote SCADA platform ties asset performance directly to operational SLAs.

Business Outcome

Sustained yield, lower operational downtime, and transparent performance reporting.

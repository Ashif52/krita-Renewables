Leadership

The team behind Krita.

Krita is led by seasoned professionals who have operated at senior levels across India's renewable energy, finance, and manufacturing sectors.

SN

Srinivasan N

DIRECTOR & CO-FOUNDER

Chartered Accountant with over three decades of post-qualification experience spanning project management, technology sourcing, team building, and resource mobilisation. Former associate with Coopers Lybrand (now PwC), A V Birla Group, and Shriram Group.

Founded Auro Mira Energy Company in 2005 — a renewable energy boutique funded by IFC, Baring Private Equity, and Aureos. Built and operated three biomass thermal plants totalling 35 MW, and an 18 MW run-of-river hydro project in Karnataka. Raised debt exceeding ₹3,000 crore for wind and solar projects.

Has hands-on experience in wind turbine manufacturing, having developed 700 kW and 1.7 MW turbines. Over a decade of experience in independent O&M across multiple turbine makes.

Parthasarathy T S

HEAD — OPERATIONS

Mechanical Engineer and MBA (Production, Quality & Transport) with over three decades of experience in production planning, supply chain management, quality systems, and project execution. Has held senior roles at Greaves Cotton, Revathy Equipment Construction, Pioneer Wincon, and Emergia Wind Turbine.

Specialises in wind turbine design, international and domestic supply chain development, and end-to-end project delivery.

Engineering-led renewable energy company delivering clean energy solutions for industries and large-scale commercial clients.

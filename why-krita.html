Krita Renewables Private Limited is a Chennai-based renewable energy company delivering reliable, scalable clean energy infrastructure exclusively for industrial and large commercial clients.

Our Story

A focused mandate for industrial growth

Krita Renewables was established with a singular mandate: delivering dependable, scalable clean energy infrastructure strictly for industrial and large commercial clients. We do not serve the residential segment.

Led by a senior team with 200+ years of combined experience across solar, wind, biomass, and small hydro — including product development, project execution, manufacturing, and supply chain — Krita brings institutional-grade capability to every engagement.

Looking to the Future

We are building for the long term — with a clear roadmap, conservative financials, and an ambition to rank among India's top-tier renewable energy companies.

Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about our renewable energy services and project implementation.

What types of industries do you serve?
+
We focus exclusively on industrial and large commercial clients. This includes manufacturing units, textile mills, cement plants, educational institutions, hospitals, and corporate parks. We do not provide services for individual residential projects.
Do you provide turnkey EPC services?
+
Yes, we provide end-to-end EPC (Engineering, Procurement, and Construction) services. This includes initial feasibility studies, detailed engineering design, procurement of high-quality components, professional installation, and final commissioning.
What is the typical ROI for an industrial solar project?
+
Most industrial rooftop solar projects in India see a payback period of 3 to 4 years. Given that the systems are designed to last 25 years, the remaining 21-22 years provide virtually free energy, significantly improving your bottom line.
How do you handle maintenance after installation?
+
We offer comprehensive O&M (Operation & Maintenance) packages that include 24/7 remote monitoring, periodic panel cleaning, electrical safety checks, and performance reporting to ensure your system operates at peak efficiency.
Can you help with financing or IPP models?
+
Yes, apart from the CAPEX model (where the client owns the plant), we also facilitate OPEX/PPA models through our investor network, where you only pay for the energy consumed without any upfront investment.
